FAQ

Common questions.

What counts as a "query"?

A query is any message sent to the Forge AI chat interface — whether freeform, a response to a guided prompt, or a follow-up. File uploads are counted separately. Free tier limits reset at midnight UTC.

Which file types are supported?

All tiers support PDF, PNG, and JPG/JPEG up to 10 MB. Forge AI can read multi-page PDFs and paginate them in the viewer. Pro and Enterprise tiers will add DXF and SVG support in a future release.

How does the RAG knowledge base work?

Your query is converted into a 1,536-dimensional vector using voyage-large-2 embeddings, then a cosine similarity search is performed against your engineering knowledge base stored in Supabase pgvector. The top-5 most relevant document chunks are retrieved and passed to Claude alongside your question — grounding answers in actual engineering literature.

Can I cancel or change plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. No long-term contracts, no lock-in.
